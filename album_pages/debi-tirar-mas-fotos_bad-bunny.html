DeBÍ TiRAR MáS FOToS
9.3/10

28.02.26 - Several-th listen over the past several months:
I really appreciated this album when I first heard it so it was great seeing it get global acclaim and recognition. It’s a beautifully complex piece of work. It has so much emotional depth whilst talking about losing one’s own home or regretting the time’s not spent with family and friends, yet it simultaneously posses such an all round positive energy that really captivates the soul. I’m a big fan of this type of patriotism, not where you scream from your lungs that “MY COUNTRY IS THE BEST!” and shove your ideas down someone’s throat, but when you quietly yet passionately strive to preserve your home. It’s such a stark juxtaposition to the ‘American’ stereotype that it’s honestly very refreshing to see just a little less hatred and a bit more love, vibes, and introspection. The production is wonderful. The composition is wonderful. The few storylines and narratives I have researched are also extremely profound and compelling, and the performance is also top quality. This album has many incredibly high highs but it also does have quite a few, ‘okay’ moments that either take away from the continuity or remove the flow. That’s why I’m not yet rating it higher, but overall it’s still an incredibly enjoyable and extremely well made body of work. Delicate 9 (9.3).
