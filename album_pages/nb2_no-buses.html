17.02.26 - First Listen
Spotify recommended this to me; I listened whilst studying. This band reminded me of Geese’s vibe except they’re more uplifting and, of course, Japanese. I really liked the sheer amount of dissonance and juxtaposition between the two lead guitars. It was really cool when there was a soft relaxing guitar melody layered beneath a screeching distorted monolith of sound. Also, the drummer was very legit. The hi-hats were so soft, yet swift; they were mesmerising to listen to. All in all I enjoyed. I like this album.

22.02.26 - Second Listen
My girlfriend said ‘Slip, Fall, Sleep’ made her feel like a japanese protagonist of sorts. I hear that.Also, I concur with my previous review. I like this album.

26.02.26 A couple listens later…
Soothing yet rebellious. Somber defiance. Provided comfort for me when conflicted internally. I like this album.

28.02.26 Thoughts
Here, ‘No Buses’ have combined a great musical hybrid of both rock and jazz esc instrumentals infused with catchy Japanese sounding melodies. The project has a cohesive and somewhat distinct sound, it’s managed to capture the essence of derivative rock and expand upon it in a way, that is, to me, positive and enjoyable. While some songs, at first, can be abrasive and catchy, the novelty can wear a bit thin and start to sound a tad repetitive. I can only go so far in my thoughts on whether the body of work is complete lyrically as, to be frank, I have not idea what’s being said. Nonetheless, from a musical and production point of view, the whole album is very well put together and well performed. There are a few lapses of flow, and of course there’s quite literally a language barrier, but overall I truly think this is a wonderful body of work. Healthy 8, (8.5).
