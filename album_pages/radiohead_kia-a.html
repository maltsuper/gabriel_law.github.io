Kid A
I really like this/10

03.06.25 - 1st Listen
'Everything In Its Right Place' puts me in a state of subtle defiance, as if I'm fugitive running from home. 'Kid A' takes me to being an adventure time character performing a contemporary robot dance around my dimly lit ultraviolet bedroom. I like the orchestral elements of 'The National Anthem'. 'How To Disappear Completely' supports the aforementioned fugitive narrative. Second half of the album blended to one sound, in my ears at least, but overall it was enjoyable. ''Kid A' would go unbelievably hard when accompanied with the right place and time. I really like this album.

23.07.26 - General Thoughts (Several Listens later)
There's something about this album which is really hard to articulate. It just takes me into a different state of mind, a different world of my own. I really like this album.
