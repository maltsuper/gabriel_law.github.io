15.08.25 - General Thoughts
This is a really calming album. When I used to listen to this during my a levels after a tiring day at school it would put me in a spectacular mood. I’d get home change into some comfy clothes, lie on my bed just as the sun is starting to tire out and soften its rays. A candle burns on my desk; my blinds hang halfway closed. Then I’d lie on my bed, which at the time was about 6 by 5 foot and was without feet, so it was in the floor, and play the album and it felt like every problem went away. The fatigue from studying and stressing all day long would be absorbed by this album. I felt like it looked after me in a way which I couldn’t do myself. I really like this album.

The previous review is so goddamn true. I love this album.

03.09.25 - Re-listen
I woke up this morning and was by no means feeling good. I was very sad, pessimistic and borderline depressive. I really didn’t want to listen to anything and just wanted to mope in silence but putting this album on reminded me why I chose to dedicate so much of my life to music. It heals me. I love this album.
