    01.03.26 - First Full Listen
    I found that whilst listening to this album I could fall in and out of it quite smoothly, meaning, I would be walking or working and when my concentration slips for a moment and my mind goes back to the music there was a lot to appreciate. This, to me at the very least, doesn’t seem like the type of album to actively and intently listen to with your full attention, but it seem to have a wonderful use when supplementing another set of actions. At times the intros and outroscould drag on for a bit, although, there didn’t seem to be much structure all together anyway. The overall project seems musically cohesive, yet as a project I don’t really know where it stands, maybe I need more context. Overall all this was an enjoyable listen. Score: TBD

    02.03.26 - Second listen
    I really like this album, I don’t quite know why, but it feels so profound, mystical, and, in many ways, rageful, but in a rather beautiful manner.

    04.03.26 - Third Listen + Rating
    This album is tuff. It makes my mind go quiet when deep down I'm yearning to just go ARGHHHHHHHH for hours on end. It's chaos is spectacularly soothing. In the future, when I'm in need of some audotiry ailment, I'll return to this album to take my mind off of the world at hand. It may not have catchy melodies or a profound set of lyrics, but if you approach this album with the right expectations, patience, and environment then it has the capability to be quite emotion-and-mind altering. 8.3
