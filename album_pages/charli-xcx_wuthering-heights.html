    5.3/10

    28.02.26/01.03.26 - 1st and 2nd Listen:
    This felt like a 50/50 split between experimental potentially genre pushing sounds and generic derivative teen angst pop music, but it didn’t really commit to either.

    04.03.26 - Rating
    As a soundtrack, for all I know this was a 10/10, BUT I'm not rating it's ability to supplement a movie; I'm rating it as a musical body of work. The instrumentals are quite consitently cool, they're different and the combination of growling synthesisers and piercing strings is very interesting. For me there's not a lot to differenciate most of the songs, about half of them all feel exactly the same, but the other half, sound like they have much more going on, for example 'Dying For You' has a nice forward moving feel to it that really draws you into the groove. All in all, however, I can only see myself coming back to at most one or two of these songs.
