The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars
I really like this/10

27.05.25 - First Listen
‘Five Years’ moved me in a way that i’m not really sure how to describe. I had no clue what the song was about bar the fact that the phrase ‘five years’ was being repeated yet the emotional effect was profound. The odd time signature / feel of the initial bars of ‘Soul Love’ piqued my interest. I found the rest of the song very enjoyable also. In a following song the phrase to the effect of ‘If I become a rock and roll star I can sleep at night’ that resonated with me. Starman was great as always. I don’t have much to say about the later songs, they were good, I just need to listen to them more. I really liked this album.

30.05.25 - Second Listen
Upon searching for meaning though the album I’ve achieved a deeper level of understanding and appreciation. The initial declaration of doom has captured my heart; the story of Ziggy Stardust has captured my mind. I’m simply enthralled by the idea of this album, an intergalactic superstar with a story and skill set far beyond our comprehension coming to us to display his talents, and despite his interstellar power he soul still depreciates with more time spent in the earthly music industry. I still really like this album.
