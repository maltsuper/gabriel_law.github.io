...I Care Because You Do

Aphex Twin

18.07.26 - General Thoughts
Felt like a erroneous collection of jarring sounds. I felt that this wall of sound wasn't used in a remotely comforting way like ambience or lofi, but rather to just make the listener feel confused and occasionally make them bop their head. I wouldn't be suprised if that was the point of the album. Either way I found it particularly difficult to understand or resonate with.
