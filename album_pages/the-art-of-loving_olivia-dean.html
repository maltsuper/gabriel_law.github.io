The Art of Loving
8/10

04.01.25 - First Listen
I read a review of this album in the financial times and wasn’t really expecting to enjoy it, but I put it on because I really like Olivia Dean’s brand image. I was pleasantly surprised, I really enjoyed it. I thought that this album was really relatable and that the songwriting was great. For some, if not quite a few, of the songs I was singing along before the song had even finished which, in my opinion, is a sign of excellent songwriting. I really like this album.

28.02.26 A few passive listens later + thoughts
This album is very cohesive, It’s very well written, and most of all it’s seems…it’s extremely catchy. For example ‘So Easy (To Fall In Love)’ is like an exemplary presentation of the whole ‘VERSE-CHORUS-VERSE-INSTRUMENTAL-CHORUS’ template. It’s so simple, yet it feels so fresh, and hip, and sassy, and quite honestly, so spectacularly girl-pop if I do say so myself. There’s also a healthy amount of variance in the mood, themes, energy, and musical instruments used throughout. It’s a wonderful display of her depth not just as an artist but also as an observer of the world around her. The start is energetic and welcoming, though towards the middle it ‘could’ *eye brow raise* start to get a better repetitive sonically, but the ending starts to slowly bring you down from the energetic pop centric vibes and put you in a more introspective, bittersweet, ‘messy’, excuse the pun, state of mind. It’s as though she’s calmly setting you down getting ready to say goodbye. This album is a great simultaneous display of her skill as a songwriter and as performer. I’m not sure how replayable the album as a whole will be, but there are definitely a few song I’d go out of my way to listen to every now and again. Honest 8. (8.0)
