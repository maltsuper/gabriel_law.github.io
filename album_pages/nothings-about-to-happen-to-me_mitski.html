Nothing's About To Happen To Me
7.3/10

01.03.26 - First Listen:
Feels like a coherent body of work. Doesn’t feel particularly new; I’ve heard this musical trope before.

02.03.26 - Second listen
Upon re-listening I started to notice more depth in the album. For most of the songs there wasn’t much to latch onto or remember them by, but throughout there were a few profound moments here and there that could incite an emotional response. Truthfully, to me it feels like well made backing music, music that you’d put on for the sole purpose of supplementing the experience of doing something else. In other words, it feels like vibe music (that is not an insult, I would consider many good albums ‘vibe music’).

02.03.26 - Third Listen
There's a lot of diversity and depth to the songwriting here. She covers many interseting topics and does it in a very nuanced, unexpected, and ultimately insightful way. Musically though, there's not much variance, for better or for worse--I think it's down to preference here. I personally don't mind the fact that all of the songs sound very similar instrumentally as this helps with immersing the listener into the world building and keeping them there for a sustained period of time. Some of the songs can start to get a bit monotonous or annoying by the end, for example, I found the counting in 'rules' to be genuinely aggitating, although I'm sure some would enjoy it. All in all, however, most of the songs on the album were interesting and enjoyable, and this album seems, to me, to be a cohesive and well made project.
